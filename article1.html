How to Remove Oil &
Grease Stains from
Clothes

Oil and grease stains are among the most common and frustrating clothing problems. But don't worry — with the right approach, you can save your favourite garments.

Step 1: Act Fast

The sooner you treat the stain, the better your chances. Blot (don't rub) excess oil with a clean paper towel.

Step 2: Apply Dish Soap

Apply a small amount of liquid dish soap directly to the stain. Dish soap is designed to cut through grease.

Step 3: Let It Sit

Leave the soap on the stain for 10–15 minutes.

Step 4: Scrub Gently

Using an old toothbrush, gently scrub the stained area in circular motions.

Step 5: Wash Normally

Wash the garment in the warmest water safe for the fabric. Check the stain before drying — heat can set the stain permanently.

Pro Tip: For old, set-in grease stains, try applying baking soda to the area first, let it absorb for 30 minutes, then follow the steps above.
